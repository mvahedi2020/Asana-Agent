# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Asana Agent

## 1. Product Positioning
**Tagline:** Your Asana copilot for high-velocity teams.
**Elevator Pitch:** Asana Agent is an intelligent conversational layer that lives right where you work. It eliminates administrative overhead by auto-prioritizing tasks, surfacing critical blockers, and executing routine updates through simple natural language commands. Stop managing your work, and start doing it.

## 2. Target Market
- **Primary Persona:** Mid-to-senior Project Managers and Scrum Masters managing 3+ concurrent projects.
- **Secondary Persona:** Individual Contributors (ICs) experiencing notification fatigue and context-switching tax.
- **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** B2B Tech companies (50-500 employees) currently paying for Asana Premium/Business but struggling with process adoption.

## 3. Competitive Differentiation
| Competitor | Their Weakness | Our Advantage |
|------------|----------------|---------------|
| Native Asana Rules | Rigid, boolean logic; hard to set up. | Natural language understanding; zero setup required. |
| Slack Slash Commands | Transactional only; no context awareness. | Deep contextual graph understanding; proactive alerts. |
| Generic LLMs (ChatGPT) | No direct access to live Asana graph. | Fully integrated read/write access to native Asana APIs. |

## 4. Pricing & Packaging Strategy
- **Freemium Tier:** Free up to 100 queries/month. (Goal: Drive bottom-up IC adoption).
- **Pro Tier:** $12/user/month for unlimited queries, advanced reporting, and bulk actions.
- **Enterprise Tier:** Custom pricing for SOC2 compliance, dedicated support, and custom model fine-tuning.

## 5. Launch Timeline
- **Phase 1 (Alpha):** Internal dogfooding (Weeks 1-2).
- **Phase 2 (Private Beta):** Invite 50 highly engaged PMs from our waitlist (Weeks 3-4).
- **Phase 3 (Public Launch):** Product Hunt launch, SEO campaign, and targeted LinkedIn ads (Week 6).

## 6. Marketing Channels
1. **Product Hunt:** Aiming for Top 3 Product of the Day. Offer a 30% discount code.
2. **Content Marketing:** Publish a "State of Project Management" report highlighting time wasted on admin tasks.
3. **Partnerships:** Get listed on the official Asana App Directory.
