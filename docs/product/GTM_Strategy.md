# GTM Strategy

This is a portfolio sample, not a launched product. A responsible path to market would begin with an internal design-partner pilot for product and engineering leads who already manage work in a structured task system.

The positioning hypothesis is “faster coordination with inspectable, reversible changes.” Initial distribution would use guided demos and an opt-in sandbox. Activation would mean completing a grounded read task and safely confirming or canceling an update. Retention would mean repeated use for weekly briefing and triage flows.

Before wider release, the team would need permission design, tenant isolation, audit logs, integration reliability, model evaluation, support playbooks, and measured evidence from consenting users. No demand, conversion, retention, or revenue result has been observed for this sample.
